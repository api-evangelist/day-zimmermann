Day & Zimmermann is pleased to announce that Jason Dunaway, president of its Maintenance and Construction division, has been appointed to the National Safety Council’s board of directors. Dunaway was elected as an at-large director and is one of five new members who will help provide strategic oversight for the organization and guide initiatives addressing some of the nation’s most pressing workplace and roadway safety challenges. He was seated during the NSC’s annual board meeting at the 2026 NSC Safety Congress & Expo in Indianapolis September 11-17.

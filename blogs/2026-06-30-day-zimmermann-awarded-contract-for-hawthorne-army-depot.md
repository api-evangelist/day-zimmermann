PHILADELPHIA, Pa. — June 26, 2026 — Day & Zimmermann (D&Z) announced today that it has been awarded a $2,301,227,487 firm-fixed-price contract by the U.S. Army for the operation, maintenance, and modernization of Hawthorne Army Depot (HWAD), Joint Munitions Command’s largest joint storage depot in Hawthorne, Nevada.

PHILADELPHIA, PA —June 2, 2026 — Day & Zimmermann (D&Z) and Deep Fission, Inc. (Deep Fission) announced today their partnership in the construction of Deep Fission’s Gravity Nuclear Reactor, the company’s proprietary underground nuclear reactor. Deep Fission is an advanced nuclear energy company developing small modular pressurized water reactors installed one mile underground.

Day & Zimmermann is a privately held, family-owned diversified industrial services company founded in 1901 and headquartered in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. The company operates across four primary business segments — Maintenance & Construction, Munitions, Government Services, and Workforce Solutions (Yoh) — and is one of the largest privately held U.S. corporations, reporting approximately $2.7 billion in revenue in 2023 with roughly 43,000–52,000 employees across approximately 150 global locations.

No public developer APIs have been published. Day & Zimmermann delivers its services through long-cycle service contracts, government-owned contractor-operated (GOCO) facilities, and direct client engagements rather than a programmable API surface.

## Ownership and History

- **Founded:** 1901 by Charles Day and Kern Dodge (originally "Dodge & Day"); John Zimmermann joined in 1907

- **Chairman & CEO:** Harold L. Yoh III
- **Headquarters:** Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
- **Notable early work:** Engineering on the Panama Canal's Gatun Lock System (1909)

## Business Segments

### Maintenance and Construction

Maintenance, construction, modification, and outage services for nuclear, fossil, and renewable power generation, plus chemical and industrial plants. Capabilities include welding, scaffolding, insulation, radiological protection, and turnkey plant services.

### Munitions

Ammunition manufacturing, loading, assembly, packing, storage, and demilitarization for the U.S. Department of Defense. Day & Zimmermann operates the **Lake City Army Ammunition Plant** in Independence, Missouri under a government-owned contractor-operated (GOCO) arrangement, producing small caliber ammunition (5.56mm, 7.62mm, .50 caliber) for the U.S. Army. Additional product lines include medium caliber rounds, warheads, fuzes, and direct/indirect fire munitions. The company is described as "one of America's leading producers of ammunition."

### Government Services

Integrated security services, explosive ordnance disposal (EOD), facility management, architecture, engineering, and commissioning for U.S. government agencies and federal installations. In 2011, Day & Zimmermann held 351 Army contracts valued at $151.8 million.

### Workforce Solutions (Yoh)

STEM staffing and talent placement operated through **Yoh** (yoh.com), a Day & Zimmermann company. Service lines include IT, engineering, life sciences, and healthcare staffing, along with RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing), MSP (Managed Service Provider), and EOR (Employer of Record) workforce models. Yoh maintains 6,000+ active contractors and 80+ years of STEM placement experience.
